## ✨ The Complete Series

### 🏰 YUNG's Better Dungeons
Complete redesign of Minecraft's dungeons. Adds **Catacombs**, **Fortresses of the Undead**, and **Spider Caves** — three new dungeon types with unique themes, mobs, and loot. Nether dungeons generate in the Nether as an optional feature.

### ⛏️ YUNG's Better Mineshafts
A long-overdue overhaul of abandoned mineshafts. Rebuilds them into sprawling, interconnected mining networks with better loot, more ore, and actual danger.

### 🏛️ YUNG's Better Strongholds
Complete redesign of strongholds with **15+ unique room types**, multiple tunnel systems, spiral staircases, traps, and hidden dark zones. Includes full Save My Stronghold functionality.

### 🏜️ YUNG's Better Desert Temples
Redesigns desert temples into massive underground complexes with puzzles, traps, and parkour. **Mining fatigue** applies inside until you defeat the **Pharaoh** boss at the end — then it's permanently lifted for that temple.

### 🌴 YUNG's Better Jungle Temples
Transforms jungle temples into vast multi-room structures filled with clever puzzles, hidden passages, and deadly traps. Built-in compatibility with Supplementaries and Pick Your Poison.

### 🧙 YUNG's Better Witch Huts
Adds overhauled witch huts to swamps. Larger, more detailed, with actual reasons to explore them.

### 🌊 YUNG's Better Ocean Monuments
Complete redesign of ocean monuments. Bigger, more complex, with better guardians and loot.

### 🔥 YUNG's Better Nether Fortresses
Complete redesign of Nether fortresses. Expanded layouts, new rooms, and improved Nether loot.

### 🌌 YUNG's Better End Island
Overhaul of the main End Island where the dragon fight takes place. New terrain, new challenges.

### 🌉 YUNG's Bridges
Adds beautiful naturally generated bridges throughout the world — connecting terrain, crossing ravines, and making the world feel more lived-in.

### 🌿 YUNG's Extras
Extra structures, features, and vanilla+ content suitable for any modpack.

### 🕳️ YUNG's Cave Biomes
Adds new vanilla+ cave biomes and underground content.

A: A collection of mods that completely redesign Minecraft's vanilla structures — dungeons, mineshafts, strongholds, temples, and more.

**Q: Forge, NeoForge, or Fabric?**
A: All three are supported. Each structure mod ships separately for Forge, NeoForge, and Fabric.

**Q: What does it require?**
A: **YUNG's API** is required for every mod in the series.

**Q: How many mods are in the series?**
A: 9+ structure overhauls plus YUNG's Bridges, Extras, and Cave Biomes.

**Q: Can I use only some mods?**
A: Yes — each mod is independent. Download only the structures you want.

A: Yes — the entire series is designed for mutual compatibility.

A: Yes — Repurposed Structures offers custom datapacks for several YUNG's mods.

A: A boss at the end of the temple. Defeating it permanently lifts the mining fatigue effect for that temple.

**Q: What are the new dungeon types in Better Dungeons?**
A: Catacombs, Fortresses of the Undead, and Spider Caves.

A: No — **Java Edition only**.
